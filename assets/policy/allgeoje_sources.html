이 앱은 공공기관의 앱이 아닙니다 / Not a government app

올거제는 개인이 만든 비공식 앱입니다. 거제시청·거제시립도서관을 비롯한 어떤 정부·공공기관과도 제휴하거나 이를 대표하지 않으며, 공공기관의 승인을 받아 운영되는 앱이 아닙니다.

AllGeoje is an unofficial app made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing Geoje City Hall, Geoje City Library, or any other government body.

These two government websites may be unreachable from outside the Republic of Korea due to the operating agency's own access policy (measured 2026-10-09: 0.2-0.7s from within Korea, timeout via overseas routes). They are not operated by AllGeoje, so their availability is outside our control.

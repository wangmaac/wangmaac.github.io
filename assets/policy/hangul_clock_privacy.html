Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android app Bitgeul (com.ghsong.hangul_clock). There is no sign-up and no advertising, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number.

  • Trial record: a SHA-256 hash of the app-specific device number (ANDROID_ID) and the trial start time are stored on our server so the 7-day trial is used once. They are sent only after you tap "Start 7 days free". The original number never leaves the device. Kept until the service ends; email us to ask for deletion.
  • City: the number of the city you choose is sent to fetch weather. It is not stored and is never sent together with the device value.
  • Approximate location: used only on the device, only when you tap "Use current location". Not stored or sent.
  • Purchase status: checked with Google Play. We never receive payment details.
  • IP address: used while handling a request to prevent abuse. Not stored.
  • Settings and last weather: kept on the device, and in your Google backup if enabled.

The server runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1) and may be outside your country. Weather comes from KMA, AirKorea and MET Norway, which receive only city coordinates or station names. Payments are handled by Google Play.

The "Alarms & reminders" permission is used only to keep the home screen widget on time. The app is not directed at children under 13.
